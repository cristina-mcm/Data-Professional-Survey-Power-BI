# Data Professional Survey Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes survey responses from data professionals to explore salaries, programming-language preferences, career-entry difficulty, and job satisfaction.

The project demonstrates the complete Power BI workflow, from preparing survey data in Power Query to presenting the results in an interactive dashboard.

## Data Preparation

The survey data was cleaned and transformed in Power Query:

- Removed unnecessary columns.
- Simplified job titles and programming-language responses.
- Converted salary ranges into numerical average salaries.
- Corrected data types for analysis and visualization.

## Key Findings

- The survey included 630 data professionals with an average age of 29.87 years.
- Data Scientists reported the highest average salary, while students and respondents still entering the field reported the lowest.
- Python was the most popular programming language by a wide margin.
- The largest group of respondents described entering the data field as neither easy nor difficult (42.7%).
- Approximately 31.7% considered entering the field difficult or very difficult, compared with 25.6% who considered it easy or very easy.
- Work-life balance received a higher average satisfaction score (5.74 out of 10) than salary satisfaction (4.27 out of 10), suggesting greater dissatisfaction with compensation.
